How it works

    
    
      
  1. Voice detection finds the speech and cuts it into pieces of up to 25 seconds.
    2. 
      
  2. Each piece is transcribed by our Whisper-medium model, fine-tuned on about 300 hours of Bangla speech.
    3. 
      
  3. On whole media recordings our fine-tuned models get about 14% of words wrong, against 26% for the model they started from.
    4. 
      
  4. Your video is deleted from the server as soon as the subtitles are made.
