Bangla ASR labeling ·

Two tasks. Your work saves automatically in this browser. Click "Download my labels" at the end of every session and send the file to Omi. Do not clear browser data, and use the same browser each time (or "Restore from file" on a new one).

Task 1: References (about 2–3 minutes per chunk)

You hear a short chunk from a long recording and see its transcript. Make the text match exactly the words that are spoken.

  • Fix real mistakes only: missing words, extra words (not spoken), wrong words.
  • Do not change spelling. If a word is spoken and the transcript writes it in a valid spelling (ছিল / ছিলো, কি / কী, কোন / কোনো), leave it as it is.
  • Dialect or colloquial speech: write what is said. If the speaker says কইরা and the text has করে, change it to কইরা. If the speaker says the standard form, keep the standard form.
  • Words cut off at the start or end: keep the word if more than half of it is audible, drop it otherwise, and tick "words cut off".
  • Numbers: write them as words, as spoken (পঁচিশ, not ২৫).
  • English words: write them in Bangla script, as the transcript does (ফোন, not phone).
  • Do not write fillers or hesitations (উম, আ…), laughter, or music.
  • Background speech: transcribe only the main speaker.
  • Can't use: choose it if you can't make out the main speech (music, noise, overlap, another language).
  • Listen as many times as you need. Don't guess: if a word is unclear, leave the original and add a note.

Choose Correct as is when you change nothing, Corrected after an edit (picked automatically when you edit), or Can't use. Ctrl+Enter saves and goes to the next chunk.

Task 2: Word pairs (about 10 seconds per pair)

Each pair is a word from a transcript (left) and the word a speech recognizer wrote instead (right), with example sentences. Decide what the difference is:

  • 1 Same word, different spelling: both are ways to write the same word with the same meaning (ছিল / ছিলো, কি / কী, রং / রঙ).
  • 2 Dialect or colloquial form: the same word, pronounced the regional or spoken way (নিয়ে / নিয়া, তোমার / তুমার, করে / কইরা).
  • 3 Different word: the meaning or grammar changes (আর / আরো, এখন / এখনো, করে / করা, বাংলা / বাংলাদেশ).
  • 4 Can't tell.

Judge the words with the help of the examples, not whether the recognizer was right. Some pairs are shared with the other annotators to measure agreement, so decide on your own.
